## 📖 Overview

**No More** is a modern-era total conversion and alternate scenario mod for *Hearts of Iron IV*. Set against the backdrop of the turbulent 2020s, the mod immerses players into a world of rising geopolitical tensions, shifting global alliances, domestic crises, and rapid political change.

From the political fallout of Brexit in the British Isles to the brewing conflicts across Europe and the globe, **No More** brings contemporary politics, modern mechanics, and unique focus trees into the grand strategy world of Hearts of Iron IV.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📅 2020 Bookmark: "Worrysome"
Start your campaign at the dawn of the new decade. Experience the escalating pressures facing modern nations, from political polarization and economic turbulence to international instability.

### 🏛️ Dual Leader & Head of Government System
Experience deeper political immersion with a custom **Second Leader GUI**! Nations can now represent both their Head of State and Head of Government (such as a President and Prime Minister / Taoiseach) simultaneously in the politics and diplomacy interfaces.

### 🗺️ Tailored Focus Trees & Dynamic Politics
- **United Kingdom:** Steer Boris Johnson's government through the post-Brexit landscape. Navigate the vision of *"Global Britain"*, manage Scottish independence pressures, and handle economic and constitutional challenges.
- **Ireland:** Guide the Republic through post-Brexit European relations, housing debates, Northern Irish reunification questions, and foreign policy neutrality.
- **Global Powers:** Expanded setups for modern superpowers and regional actors including the United States, Russia, Ukraine, France, Germany, and China.

### 🎨 Custom Visuals & Modern Assets
- High-quality portraits for contemporary world leaders, ministers, and advisors.
- Custom GUI elements, loading screens, and thematic UI additions tailored for modern grand strategy.

## 📥 Installation

### Manual Installation (Development / GitHub)
1. Download or clone this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/boredguy7773/No-More-Hoi4-Mod.git
   ```
2. Place the mod directory into your Paradox Interactive mod folder:
   - **Windows:** `Documents\Paradox Interactive\Hearts of Iron IV\mod\nomore`
3. Ensure `descriptor.mod` (or a `.mod` file pointing to the directory) is present.
4. Open the **Paradox Launcher**, add the mod to your active Playset, and launch the game!

---

## ⚙️ Compatibility & Recommended Settings

- **Game Version:** Hearts of Iron IV `1.14+` / latest compatible update.
- **DLCs:** Compatible with base game; major DLCs (such as *Man the Guns*, *No Step Back*, *By Blood Alone*) enhance mechanics and flavor where applicable.
- **Mod Compatibility:** As a major scenario mod modifying bookmarks and UI, running alongside other total conversion or overhaul mods is not recommended.
